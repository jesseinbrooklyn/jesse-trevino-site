Trade prospecting pipeline

An outreach system for reaching interior designers, architects, builders and realtors, built so cold prospects stay out of the company CRM until they actually reply.

Role
Designed, built and run it
Status
Piloted on my own outreach
Triggered by
Feedback from sales leadership

The problem

My first round of trade outreach created a project in the CRM for every prospect up front, about 40 at a time. Most never replied, so the system filled up with dead projects. Sales leadership asked for a cleaner CRM, and it was a fair point: the clutter made the real pipeline harder to read for everyone.

I still needed to reach a lot of people, follow up on schedule, and never cold-email someone who was already a client.

What I built

A separate prospect list that lives outside the CRM, with an assistant that tracks every send, reply and follow-up. A prospect only becomes a CRM project once they respond, and on that day the full email history goes with them, so the record is complete from the start.

363prospects moved out of spreadsheets into one tracked list
97existing clients caught before they got a cold email, 9 of them hidden among "new" prospects

How it works

  1. Check first. Every prospect is checked against the client list. Anyone who is a client, or hasn't been checked yet, can't be emailed.
  2. Daily scan. Each morning the assistant reads the inbox and sorts what came back: real replies, out-of-office notes, bounces and unsubscribe requests.
  3. Follow-up on a schedule. Each send sets the next touch: day 10, then day 31, then quarterly. Due follow-ups are drafted in batches for me to review.
  4. Promote on reply. When someone responds, a checklist creates the CRM project, attaches the email history, confirms it landed and sets the first reminder. Every email ends up logged in the CRM.

Decisions that mattered

  • Start from the feedback. The whole design starts from one rule leadership cared about: nothing enters the CRM until it's a real lead.
  • A skipped scan is never "no replies." If email is down, the scan says so loudly instead of reporting a quiet inbox.
  • Statuses only move forward. A prospect who replied can't be accidentally reset to "new," and an unsubscribe is permanent.
  • Earn each step. Automatic follow-ups were added only after the scanner ran cleanly for a week.
  • Contact details stay private. The prospect list never goes into version control.
