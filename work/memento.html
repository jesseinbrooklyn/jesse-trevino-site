Side project · Building

Memento Mori

A phone app for a daily Stoic practice: a morning intention, an evening reflection, and an honest reminder that time is limited.

Role
Concept, design and build
Status
Version 1 in testing
Platform
iOS and Android

Why I made it

Most habit apps reward streaks and badges. I wanted the opposite: something quiet that asks a hard question each morning and holds you to an answer by evening. It's also where I practice design craft without a client brief, from the typography to the illustration.

What it does

  1. Morning. The app opens on an estimate of your remaining days, and you set one intention for the day.
  2. Evening. Reflection unlocks at 5 PM. You can't close out the day early.
  3. Practice grid. A 30-day dot grid fills in only when both the morning and evening steps are done.
  4. Wisdom. A library of 100 classical quotes, from Marcus Aurelius and Seneca to Viktor Frankl, with a personal collection of favorites.

Design choices

  • A Renaissance look. Dark, warm palette, classical type and a vanitas painting behind the interface, so it feels like an object, not a dashboard.
  • Private by design. Everything stays on your phone. There's no account and no cloud.
  • Time as a rule. The morning and evening steps unlock by the clock, so the app shapes a routine instead of waiting to be opened.
