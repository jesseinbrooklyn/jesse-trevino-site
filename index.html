About

I'm a design consultant at The Shade Store's Paramus Design Center, where I guide clients from first measure to final install. I see firsthand how sales, production and installation teams hand work to each other, and where those handoffs slow down.

Along the way I started building small tools to take repetitive work off my plate. That habit grew into a real interest in how projects run, and how good systems let people spend their time on the work that matters.

I'm self-taught, and I work like a forward-deployed engineer: I sit with the people doing the work, find where it slows down, and build the tooling right there. For now most of it is used by me and my team, which is where I find out what's worth scaling.
